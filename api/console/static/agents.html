Agents you allowed through their MCP sign-in. Each one manages and uses the boxes it created, within the limits you set, and the boxes of yours you chose for it; it never sees your other boxes or shares. The boxes it creates get none of your saved keys or connected accounts. On a box you choose, it acts as you: the box's saved keys are injected into its outbound requests, and it can use the box's connected accounts, browser logins and files; it never deletes that box. A change of chosen boxes applies to its next call. Revoking one ends its access at once; the boxes it created stay yours.
