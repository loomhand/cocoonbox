Sharing

    
A share link shows the box's whole desktop, including the browser's signed-in sites and /workspace, to anyone who holds it, or only to the users you name. Their own agents can connect over MCP: read-only, with files only under /workspace, or acting on the box when you allow write. Box egress uses your model key; a guest's own agent spends the guest's quota. With write, programs a guest runs in the box can use your keys on their hosts, as your own can.
