If you allow it, it gets your whole account, as your personal token does: every box, your keys, your connected accounts and your shares. It stays signed in until you revoke it under Devices on the console, or sign it out with cocoonbox logout.

asks to manage cocoonbox boxes for you

If you allow it, it may create boxes, run programs in them, and list, wake, save, upgrade, hibernate and delete only the boxes it creates. Their programs use your model budget. It cannot see or touch your other boxes or your shares, and this agent cannot use your saved keys or connected accounts: the boxes it creates get neither. Its boxes do not count against your own box limit: the limits below bound them, and they hibernate when no one uses them. You can revoke it on the Agents page at any time; its boxes then stay yours.
